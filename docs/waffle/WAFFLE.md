# Waffle

Waffle is laid out like Windows 11: a taskbar along the bottom, a Start menu, an Action Center and a Notification
Center. Everything here is set in **Waffle Settings**, which opens with **Super+,**.

<!-- preview shot=waffle-start -->

## On your screen

- **Taskbar**: along the bottom, or the top. **Waffle Settings › Taskbar** sets its icons, the clock, Desktop Peek and
  the update badge.
- **Start menu**: one sheet with a round search, your pinned tiles and the apps open now as cards. Click a card to go
  back to that window. Files and Settings sit next to Power. **All apps** lists every app from A to Z: click a letter
  to jump through the alphabet, right-click an app to pin it to the taskbar. **Waffle Settings › Waffle Style › Start
  Menu** sets its layout.
- **Action Center**: wide pills that show the state of each toggle, and thick volume and brightness bars that fill with
  your accent. Night Light sits next to brightness.
- **Notification Center**: today on an accent tile, a Focus pill, the notification history and the calendar.
- **Notifications**: each toast starts with its app on a tile. A critical one has a red edge.
- **On-screen display**: a capsule with the icon on an accent well, and the name and percent over a thick bar. Muted
  sound turns it gray.
- **Widgets panel**: a board where each widget is its own card: weather, media, the calendar and the widgets you add
  from the Hub.
- **Task View**: the desktop sinks behind frosted glass. Each workspace wears a name chip, and the search is a pill.
- **Alt+Tab**: cards on one frosted pane, each with its app and title on top. The picked card shows its window live.
- **Lock screen**: a bold white clock over a soft veil, readable on any wallpaper.
- **Power screen** (**Super+Shift+Q**): you on frosted glass over big tiles to lock, sleep, sign out, restart or shut
  down. Task Manager and the firmware settings sit below. The arrows or Tab move, Enter chooses, Esc goes back.

## Where things are in Waffle Settings

Pages are listed on the left: Quick, General, Taskbar, Background, Themes, Gowall, Interface, Modules, Waffle Style,
Shortcuts, About, Monitors, Autostart, Workspace Strip, Mascot, AI, Effects, Shell Layout and Hub.
**Ctrl+PageDown** and **Ctrl+PageUp** move between them, and **Ctrl+F** searches every option.

- **General**: audio, battery, time and language, idle and sleep, Game Mode and the calendar.
- **Background**: the wallpaper, one per monitor, transitions, effects and the overview backdrop.
- **Interface**: display scaling, notifications, the floating tools (**Super+G**), the on-screen display and the lock
  screen.
- **Waffle Style**: Alt+Tab, Task View, Start, the Widgets panel, weather and the calendar.
  In Alt+Tab, Tab and the arrows move the pick and the window switches when you let go of Alt or press Enter; Esc
  leaves focus where it was. With Alt still held, the auto-hide delay switches to the pick on its own.
- **Gowall**: recolours an image with your palette, pixelates or upscales it.

## Its looks

**Waffle Settings › Themes** picks the colour theme. Waffle keeps its own layouts; with **Take style from Material**
on (the default) it also wears Material's global style (Aurora glass, Angel, Editorial type...) and its transparency,
chosen right below. Switch it off and Waffle is plain Fluent whatever Material uses. Colours follow the wallpaper unless you pick a theme: [Colours and themes](THEMING_PRESETS.md).
