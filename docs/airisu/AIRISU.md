# AIRISU

AIRISU is a Design for iRiS: the same shell in lacquer. Urushi black and vermilion over the panels you know, figures
that lean like a brush stroke, and an Island that stamps the day as a seal.

## Wear it

1. Settings › Style › Design › More in the Hub, or `inir hub install airisu`.
2. Settings › Style › Design, then AIRISU.

Pick iRiS there to take it off: your own look comes back as you left it. `inir hub remove airisu` removes it; if you
were wearing it, iRiS shows again.

## What it changes

- Colour: its own lacquer palette, the night preset. Your scheme (dark, ink or light) still applies.
- Type: Archivo SemiExpanded for titles and figures, leaning forward; its kana face for the seal.
- Motion: tuned, a little slower than iRiS.
- The Island at rest: the weekday's kanji over the date as a seal, beside the hour; the weather small after it. A song,
  a timer or a task takes the strip in turn; a notice, a level or a recording lacquers the whole strip.

Everything else (where things are, what shows, your wallpaper, your material and texture) stays yours. While you wear
AIRISU, the rows it decides leave Settings.
