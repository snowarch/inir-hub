# iRiS

iRiS is the Island family. One Island sits on a screen edge and grows into whatever you open, while the Dock, the pieces and the desktop widgets follow the same look.

## The Island

<!-- preview shot=iris-edge -->

- It rests on the top, bottom, left or right edge: iRiS Settings › Island › Layout › Screen edge.
- It opens into pages for music, activity, the shelf, the desktop and more.
- Island layout (Settings › Island › Layout › Island layout):
  - **Island** keeps it in the middle of the edge.
  - **Corner left** and **Corner right** dock it in one corner, melted into both walls.
  - **Shelf** spans the whole edge, the Island rising from it.
  - **Pods** sets three bodies on one line that melt where they meet.
  - **Menu bar** makes a thin strip with the Island hanging from it as a notch. Its strip is Transparent or Band (Menu bar strip).
  - The Island design you wear shows in every layout. While a song plays, its cover and its moving bars sit at the design's sides and the design stays in the middle; paused, the cover dims and stays.
- Island shape (Settings › Island › Shape › Island shape): Auto, Round, Squircle or Square.
- Island design (Settings › Island › Design and modes) sets how the resting Island looks: each design is shown alive in a small Island before you pick it. Composition and Island layout live in the same group. Besides Halo, Ledger, Tiles, Dial, Marquee and Duo:
  - **Headline**: the time tall and condensed like a poster, beside the place, the sky and the temperature.
  - **Rings**: three rings for the day gone, the battery (the week on a desktop) and the month, beside the time.
  - **Aura**: the Island ringed in the colours of the spectrum; they turn once when the pointer is over it.
  - **Comet**: one light runs round the rim with its tail fading behind it. Where its head stands counts the hour, the day or the battery, read clockwise from the top. It moves once a minute and runs a lap while the pointer is over the Island.
  - **Lumen**: the rim lit from below in two colours, like a lamp under glass, with a little of the light pooled at its foot. The light says something: the battery (its colour, and how far up the sides it climbs as it charges) or the hour's sky. Under the pointer it climbs the whole rim.
  - **Status**: a status bar: the time, the connection (cable, Wi-Fi bars or none), a red mark when the sound is off, and the battery with its figure. Without a battery it shows a connected headset's charge, else the weather, else the day.
  - **Forecast**: the time, then the next hours the way a weather app lays them out: now first, each hour its sky in colour and its temperature. It moves on once an hour. Without weather it shows the day.
  - **World**: the time here and in another city: a small watch with a light face by day and a dark one by night, the city's name over its time, how many hours apart it is, and a +1 when it is already tomorrow there.
- Settings › Island › Face holds the options of the design you wear, and only those:
  - **Ledger**: how far ahead it looks for the next event (3, 12 or 24 hours).
  - **Tiles**: which tile is lit (the hour, the minutes or both) and whether the day sits beside them.
  - **Dial**: the marks round the watch (every hour, quarters or none) and what sits beside it (the day, the time in figures or nothing).
  - **Marquee**: whether the next event, the weather and the sunrise or sunset take their turn; the date always stays.
  - **Duo**: whether the temperature shows in the window's corner.
  - **Headline**: the place and the sky, or the day and the date, beside the time; and the temperature at the end.
  - **Rings**: what each ring counts: the day, the week, the month, the year or the battery.
  - **Aura**: its colours (Spectrum, your accent, the day's sky, the wallpaper, Sunset, Ocean or Mint) and whether they turn under the pointer.
  - **Comet**: what its head counts (the hour, the day or the battery), a Tail behind it or the whole Track lit, the same colours as Aura, and the lap under the pointer.
  - **Lumen**: what its light tells (the battery, the sky or one of Aura's colours) and the light at its foot.
  - **Status**: what its capsule carries (Auto, the battery, a headset, the sky or the day), the connection mark and the red sound-off mark.
  - **Forecast**: how many hours it shows (2, 3 or 4, now included; a narrow Island shows fewer), each hour's name, and the sky in colour or in grey.
  - **World**: the city (50 to pick from, searchable), a second city beside it, the hours apart and the small watch. Short of room the hours apart step out first, then the watches, then the second city, so no name or time is ever cut.
  - **Halo**: Sunlight, its glow where the sun stands.
- Live activities look like a phone's Dynamic Island:
  - **Music** opens into a player with the cover, the song and its artist, the time on both sides of the bar and large play controls.
  - **Timers** show an orange ring that empties as the time runs; open, they have round pause and close buttons, and a pomodoro shows each focus and break as a segment.
  - **Downloads and tasks** show a ring with their percentage.
  - Settings › Island › Live activities has a switch for each kind that may take the Island: screen recording, timers, downloads and tasks, camera, calls and music. With **Camera** or **Calls** off, the privacy dot still shows while an app uses them. **Microphone glow** turns off the glow on the screen's edges when an app starts listening.
  - **Order** lists the same kinds first to last: drag one to move it. When several run at once, the first takes the Island and the next waits in the bubble beside it. A kind switched off keeps its place, greyed. Under Cluster the song stays in its bubble wherever it stands.
- Settings › Island › Layout › **Size** sets the Island's height, clock size and breathing room together: **Compact**, **Regular** or **Large**. Height, Clock size and Breathing room still fine-tune each one; a larger clock widens the resting Island with it.
- The Weather page shows the temperature large, the place and the sky, and the next hours on a bar and as coloured columns by how warm each hour is.
- The Vitals page shows processor, memory, heat and disk as four rings, and the processor over the last minute as a chart.
- Notices: the Island says by itself what just happened, for a moment (Settings › Island › Notices):
  - **Size**: **Compact** is a line in the resting Island (on a full-width bar or a menu bar it hangs under the strip); **Large** grows the Island into a card, as a phone's alerts do. On a side edge it stays a line.
  - **Stays for** sets how long a notice holds the Island, from 1.5 to 8 seconds. **See one** shows a notice with what you chose, and every switch shows its own notice as you turn it on.
  - Under **Fine-tune** each kind has its switch: unlock (a face drawn in green), Silent (a red pill when the sound is muted from anywhere), Do not disturb, low battery, screenshots (the picture itself; a click opens it), recordings with their length, upcoming events (a ring closing on the event, first said 10, 5 or 1 minute before), rain and snow on the way, timers and each focus or break, finished downloads, copies and updates, what you keep on the Shelf, an error in Niri's config, and loud jumps in volume held back.
  - A percentage shows only for a level (a headset's charge, the battery); an event or a finished job shows its ring.
- While the camera is on, the Island shows a green camera tile with the app.
- When an app starts using the microphone, the edges of the screen glow in colour for three seconds.
- While an app uses the microphone (a call), the Island shows a green handset, how long the call has run and the sound moving.
- Notifications open from the Island as a phone's do: the picture or app round at the start, the app small over the title. Two answers that refuse and take (a call, an invite) are a red and a green button; other answers are capsules, the last one filled. Apps that take a written answer show **Reply**: type and press Enter.
- With more than one player, dots under the controls switch between them; a swipe on the cover or the wheel sideways does too, and a click on the cover brings the app forward.
- The Bluetooth card shows the charge of every connected device as a gauge with its name.
- The calendar shows the next thing today on a coloured band with how long until it, and each entry with its colour and how far off it is.
- The Desktop page has a **Workspaces** block (Settings › Island › Desktop page › Blocks): this screen's workspaces as cards, each with its windows' icons, its name and what holds it. A tap goes there, the key on a card brings the focused window to it, and the round key at the end opens the overview. More cards than fit scroll sideways.

## Pieces and the Dock

<!-- preview shot=iris-dock -->

Pieces are the small things around the Island: weather, notifications, sound, the microphone, timers, Now Playing, the tray, calendar, battery, network, vitals and more. Each piece has its own switch and a spot under Settings › Bubbles & Cards › Extra bubbles. The spots are the four corners, the left and right edges, or Free, which floats it on the desktop.

The Dock is set under iRiS Settings › Dock:

- Visibility: Show dock, Automatically hide, Reserve space for windows, Stay visible on empty workspaces.
- Look › Screen edge: Auto puts it on the edge opposite the Island, or pick Top, Bottom, Left or Right.
- Look › Material: iRiS, Solid, Glass or Blur (beta). Glass, the factory pick, blurs your wallpaper and costs little; Blur needs Niri 26.04 or later and asks Niri to blur whatever is behind on every frame, which weaker GPUs feel.
- Apps: Pinned apps, Open apps and Drag to reorder. Drag an icon along the Dock to reorder it; **Esc** while dragging puts it back.

## Look, colour and themes

- Style › Material: the surface under every iRiS panel. Black, Graphite, Midnight, Wallpaper or Theme.
- Style › Texture: how every body is drawn, in iRiS and AIRISU alike. Ricelin is a card lit from above, Aurora thin and translucent, Angel a bright glass edge, Regalia a solid tile, Flat a plain plate; Afterglow, Light leak and Light streak bring their own light. Light streak lights everything from under the Island: a hot line on every edge that faces it, your colours rising off those edges like an aurora (Aurora, Your colours or Wallpaper), and a trail behind whatever moves; Light, Aurora and Trails set how much of each.
- Colour › Look: Apple, Nothing OS, Synth or Gel.
- Colour › Scheme: Auto, Dark mode, Ink or Light mode. Ink sits between dark and light, like paper and ink.
- Style › Colour theme: Catppuccin, Rosé Pine, Tokyo Night, Nord and more. Match the shell also gives iRiS the theme's accent, highlight and material.
- Style › Design: iRiS, or a Design from the Hub. A Design is another iRiS: its own colour, type, corners, edges, shadow and motion, and the Island's face at rest, over the same panels; nothing moves and nothing changes what it does. The last tile, More in the Hub, lists the Designs you can install. While you wear one, the rows it decides leave Settings; take it off (pick iRiS) or remove it, and your own look is back as you left it. AIRISU is the first: iRiS in lacquer, whose Island stamps the day as a seal beside the leaning hour.
- Style › Presets: the first tile on Style, beside Design and Look (the three picks that change everything). It says which look you wear; open it for every curated look as a little widget of itself (its plate, its accent, its type, its colours as a strip): one click puts the whole look on, Undo takes it back. Colour lives on Style too, in its own band, then Material, Shape and type, More. Use Customize, Themes, Save current to keep the look you have as your own theme.
- Customize: right-click the desktop and pick Customize iRiS. The Island grows a capsule with Themes, Look and Pieces, plus undo, redo and Done.
- Style › Customize opens: On the shell, which edits in place, or Studio, a side panel with search.
- Style › Icons: iRiS draws every mark as its widgets head themselves, the glyph alone in its area's colour; Icon pack brings back plates (iRiS) or app tiles. Icons › Colour: Areas colours by family. Each sidebar group has its own band: Appearance violets, Island to Control Center blues, Spotlight to Anime greens, Notifications to Login warm, Home and the foot grey. Accent paints every icon your accent; Mono keeps them grey.
- Home › Every day: the Edit button on its title picks which settings Home pins.
- Lists in Settings give a little at their ends, with a mouse wheel too, and spring back.

## Anime

- Colour layer (Settings › Anime): the Anime colour layer switch, then Palette (Sakura, Neo Tokyo, Unit-01, Magical Girl or Spirit Forest), Strength (0 keeps your own accent, 100 is the full palette) and Recolour the highlight.
- Airing (Settings › Anime › Airing): the Airing bubble switch, and how many episodes its card lists, from 3 to 8.
- Continue (Settings › Anime › Continue): watch anime without a terminal. It needs `ani-cli`, and the row says so when it is missing. Set Audio (Subtitled or Dubbed), Quality (Best, 1080p, 720p or 480p), Subtitle size, Subtitle height and Skip intros here.

## Wallpaper, desktop and Spotlight

- Wallpaper › Wallpaper gallery › Picker: Gallery is iRiS's own, out of the Island; Grid, Coverflow and Launcher open Material's pickers instead, from the same shortcut, piece or Island button.
- Wallpaper › Wallpaper gallery › Live wallpaper downloads: Best takes the 4K file when there is one, Light takes HD.
- Desktop widgets rest a little inside the frame (or the screen edge without one) and settle into its corners with the same gap.
- Widgets have a second way to read in their sheet, under Face:
  - **Clock**: Hour (the time as large as the plate lets it, over the day as one line with sunrise and sunset) or Greeting (the time, the date, and a word for the part of the day with the temperature beside it).
  - **Calendar**: Week (today and the days ahead as columns, events where they fall).
  - **Vitals**: Bars (every reading a meter, the highest leads).
  - **Screen Time**: Ribbon (the 24 hours, each coloured by the app that held it).
  - **Weather**: Week (now beside its sky, then the coming days as columns, each its sky and its high over its low; today on its own plate).
  - **Battery**: Cells (the charge as a battery of cells, the one filling breathes while it charges; other devices under it with their own small cells).
  - **Now Playing** (medium and large): Card (the player's name and the hour on top; a card with the cover beside the line being sung, or the song's bars without lyrics; then the title, the artist, the timeline and the keys, play in the song's colour).
- Under Colour › Look › Nothing OS, **Figures** draws large numbers as Nothing's dot matrix (the default) or in Archivo's wide figures.
- Arranging the desktop, a widget's sheet has three pages: Widget (what it shows), Look (background, corners, ink) and Arrange (scale, position, lock). The background key on the widget's capsule and Look › Surface are the same switch: None takes the plate off, Glass, Solid and Tinted say what it is made of, and Shared follows Style › Widgets.
- Side Panels: pick Focus or Today and the page shows that panel's sections, width, height and look, with the panel itself on the stage. Sections are a list you reorder right there.
- Desktop › Widgets › Lume on every widget: every widget's text reads as if the wallpaper behind it were bright and busy.
- Spotlight: type `/` to list iRiS's own switches, including Frame Music and Edge Music.

## Lock, capture, windows, Spotlight and Now Playing

- **Lock Screen**: what it shows (scene, clock, at a glance, now playing, activity), how you sign in and when it locks on
  its own (Security, When idle). **Super+Alt+L** locks.
- **Screenshots & Recording**: **Super+Shift+S** opens the snip; the page sets the snip, Read text (the language it
  reads), recording and the Japanese lookup.
- **Windows**: Niri's gaps, borders, corners and movement, the glass behind apps and the room windows leave for iRiS.
- **Spotlight**: search for apps, files, the clipboard and more; the page sets results, the clipboard, prefixes and web search.
- **Now Playing**: music on the Island, the lock screen and the frame: Frame music, the edge wave, the bubble, song
  naming, the visualizer and the player.

## Settings

Open Settings with Super+,. It appears over the desktop or as a window: the button beside the close button switches, as does Home › Settings window › Opens as. The layout is Classic, Folio, Pocket or Drawer, from the switch in Settings' head or Home › Settings window › Layout. The history key beside that switch, or Home › Settings window › Original design, draws Settings as it looked in v2.33 (marks on coloured plates, each area's groups in one list, a sidebar without captions) with every page and option of today still in it; press it again to come back. Drawer's head has a key for Home, one for each kind of area (Appearance, Island & Dock and so on) and one each for Sources and the Community Hub; the kind you are in spells out its areas by name. Every layout shows an area as one page: its groups open, each control in its own row, with a live preview where the area has one. Rows people rarely touch wait behind Fine-tune at the foot of their group, and groups that are all fine-tuning (corners or material per surface, say) wait together behind one Fine-tune at the foot of the area.

The sidebar lists these areas, in this order:

- Home: date and time, language, startup, terminal, the Settings window, performance and the shell family
- Style, Colour, Wallpaper, Motion
- Island, Dock, Bubbles & Cards
- Desktop, Widgets, Windows, Side Panels, Control Center
- Spotlight, Orbit, Screenshots & Recording
- Now Playing, Anime
- Notifications, Sound & Feedback
- Display, Keyboard & Mouse, Battery, Gaming & Fullscreen
- Lock Screen, Login Screen
- Sources, Community Hub

Ctrl+F searches every option. Ctrl+Up and Ctrl+Down move between areas. Back and forward are the mouse side buttons, Alt+Left and Alt+Right, or Ctrl+[ and Ctrl+].
