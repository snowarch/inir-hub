# NiHiL

A desktop family for iNiR built as panes of glass in front of the wallpaper: a floating bar on any edge of the screen,
and beside it one dashboard that holds every page (apps, controls, today, media, focus, clipboard and wallpapers).
Everything chosen wears one accent, and every part of it can be set.

## Using it

Pick NiHiL in the family switcher. Click NiHiL's logo on the bar to open the dashboard, or the clock, the status or the
power key for their own pages; Alt and a number move between pages. Press Super to open the overview and type to
search apps, windows and files.

## Options

Settings holds every option, and its search reaches all of them.

- **Profiles**: four whole desktop layouts to wear, overwrite with your own and restore.
- **Layout**: the bar (edge, floating or attached, thickness, which part goes in which zone, how many windows and tray icons), the dock (edge, size, swell, previews), the dashboard (size, sidebar, dim), dash, workspace strip, workspace
  preview, search and workspace switcher.
- **Appearance**: the accent, the glass, shape, type and the overview's background.
- **Behavior**: the overview, Super key, hot corner, windows, dash actions, motion and windows asking for attention.
- **App grid**: pages, icons, order, folders and search results.
- **Modules**: each surface on or off, and the volume popup, notifications and lock screen.
