# NiHiL

A desktop family for iNiR built as one sheet of black glass whose edge is a line of light. The bar, the app launcher, the
quick panels, the power menu and the lock screen are all that sheet changing shape, and every part of it can be set.

## Using it

Pick NiHiL in the family switcher. Press Super to open the overview, type to search apps, windows and files, and open
Settings from the panel or the overview to change anything.

## Options

Settings holds every option, and its search reaches all of them.

- **Profiles**: four whole desktop layouts to wear, overwrite with your own and restore.
- **Layout**: the panel, dash, workspace strip, workspace preview, search and workspace switcher.
- **Appearance**: the light, the glass, shape, type and the overview's background.
- **Behavior**: the overview, Super key, hot corner, windows, dash actions, motion and windows asking for attention.
- **App grid**: pages, icons, order, folders and search results.
- **Modules**: each surface on or off, and the volume popup, notifications and lock screen.
