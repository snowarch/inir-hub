# AIRISU

iRiS in lacquer: urushi black and vermilion over iRiS's own panels, figures that lean like a brush stroke, and an
Island that stamps the day as a seal (the weekday's kanji over the date) beside the hour.

- Wear it: Settings › Style › Design, then AIRISU. Pick iRiS there to take it off.
- What it sets (`design.json`): the lacquer palette and the night preset, Archivo SemiExpanded leaning for titles and
  figures, tuned motion. Its Island face and seal are its own parts; everything else is iRiS's, dressed.
- Remove it with `inir hub remove airisu`: iRiS shows its own look again, and your settings are untouched.

The kana face (`assets/fonts/AirisuKana-Bold.otf`) is a subset of Noto Sans CJK, under the SIL Open Font License
(`assets/fonts/OFL-NotoSansCJK.txt`).
