# Prism

iRiS in the language of Prism, its Settings: the three near-blacks and Apple's label inks, white solids for what is
chosen, squircle marks, rows numbered like a chart, a hairline of light on every edge, and calm motion. At rest the
Island's hour is the prism: a thread of white light meets it and leaves as the spectrum.

- Wear it: Settings › Style › Design, then Prism. Pick iRiS there to take it off.
- What it sets (`design.json`): Prism's neutrals in dark, ink and light, white solids (black ones in light) for filled
  controls and today's date, Prism's corners and squircle pieces, the edge of light, glide motion, and the Prism faces
  of the desktop widgets. Your accent, highlight, fonts, glass and wallpaper stay yours; your accent still marks where
  you are.
- A widget you gave a design of its own keeps it. Taking Prism off gives every widget your own design back.
- Remove it with `inir hub remove prism`: iRiS shows its own look again, and your settings are untouched.
