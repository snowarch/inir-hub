# Prism

iRiS in the language of Prism, its Settings: the three near-blacks and Apple's label inks, white solids for what is
chosen, round marks, bodies that stand off by their fill, and quick motion. Its glass keeps a smoked near-black over any
wallpaper. At rest the Island shows the hour over a short line of the spectrum, which opens under the pointer.

- Wear it: Settings › Style › Design, then Prism. Pick iRiS there to take it off.
- What it sets (`design.json`): Prism's neutrals in dark, ink and light, white solids (black ones in light) for filled
  controls and today's date, Prism's corners and round pieces, a smoked tint under glass, quick motion, and the Prism
  faces of the desktop widgets with their light split into the spectrum. Your accent, highlight, fonts, glass and
  wallpaper stay yours; your accent still marks where you are.
- A widget you gave a design of its own keeps it. Taking Prism off gives every widget your own design back.
- Remove it with `inir hub remove prism`: iRiS shows its own look again, and your settings are untouched.
