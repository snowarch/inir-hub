# Radio

Live stations from anywhere in the world. Press play: it finds a station somewhere playing a genre you like, or
anything at all. Next takes you to another place. A station in Santiago playing shoegaze, one in Plovdiv playing dream
pop, one in Kanagawa playing city pop: real stations, live, mostly ones you would never have found.

| Family | Where it shows | How to add it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Material | A card on the desktop | Edit the desktop, add a widget, Radio |
| iRiS | A player on the Island's Desktop page: logo, station, volume, previous, play, next; and the desktop card in iRiS, Prism or Material | The Island's Desktop page, add, Radio; edit the desktop, add a widget, Radio |
| Waffle | A card in the Widgets panel | It shows in the panel once installed |

## Using it

- **Play**: the round button (the shape on the desktop). It looks for a station, tunes in and plays.
- **Stop**: the same button. Pressing play again tunes back in live.
- **Next**: another station, often in another country.

The card shows the station, its flag and place, and what the station says plays now. Closing the Island or the
Widgets panel keeps the music playing; every face steers the same radio.

## Settings

| Setting | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| Genres you like | Genres with commas (`city pop, jazz, bossa nova`). Each tune picks one of them. Empty: any genre from a wide list, any country |
| Volume | 0 to 100. Default: 60 |

On the desktop, edit mode also picks the Shape or Instrument look and the shape the button takes while it plays.
Under iRiS the card wears the family's widget designs like every other widget: iRiS, Prism or Material, picked on
the Look page of its sheet, on the same glass and blur.
The desktop card has three sizes, picked in edit mode like any widget's: small keeps play, the tuner and next with the
station under them; medium adds the place, what plays now, the star and the volume; large adds your stations
(favourites first, then what you played lately), each one click away.

## What it needs

`mpv`, which iNiR installs. With `mpv-mpris` installed, the station also shows in the shell's media controls.
Stations come from [Radio Browser](https://www.radio-browser.info), an open directory run by volunteers. Talk
stations (news, sport, religion) are left out.
