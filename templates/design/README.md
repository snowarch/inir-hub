# __NAME__

A Design for iRiS: what the shell becomes when someone wears it, in two sentences (its idea, what people remember).

- Wear it: Settings › Style › Design, then pick its picture. Settings › Style › Design › the reset mark takes iRiS back.
- What it sets: the values in `design.json` (shapes, edges, motion…). Everything else stays the person's.
- Remove it with `inir hub remove __ID__`: iRiS shows its own look again, and the person's settings are untouched.
