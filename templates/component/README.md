# __NAME__

What it adds to the desktop, in two sentences: what someone sees, and when they would open it.

- Open it with the mouse from its widget in your family. Extras: `inir __ID__ toggle`, or a key in niri's config.
- Worn with: every family (or the ones `plugin.json` lists in `families`).
- Its options live in `~/.config/inir/plugins/__ID__.json`.
